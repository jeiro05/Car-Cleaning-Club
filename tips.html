Interior Cleaning

  • Vacuuming seats & carpets

    Use a handheld vacuum or shop vac to remove dirt, crumbs, and dust from seats and floor mats. Only use car shampoo mixed with water and a brush when floor mats are heavily dirty.

  • Dashboard dusting & polishing

    Wipe with a microfiber cloth and apply a non-greasy polish to protect against UV damage and cracking.

  • Window cleaning and de-hazing

    Spray glass cleaner and wipe in circular motions to remove haze, streaks and enhance vision.

  • Proper Usage of Microfiber Towels

    Fold the cloth into quarters and use a clean side for each wipe to avoid streaks. Make it a habit of separating towels for interior and exterior.

  • Good Car Scent

    In addition to being clean, an interior that smells good also adds well to the whole detail.

Exterior Cleaning

  • Proper car washing techniques

    Use one bucket for car shampoo with water and another for rinsing to prevent dirt scratching your paint.

  • Tire and wheel care

    Scrub wheels with a dedicated brush and cleaner to remove brake dust and grime. Tire dressing is recommended after.

  • Proper drying

    Use microfiber towels to dry the car thoroughly, preventing water spots.

  • Waxing & polishing for shine

    Use a Carnauba infused wax, better if it also repels water for a hydrophobic effect. And to give the paint a deep gloss and shine. It will also make the paint slick and car show-ready look.

  • Protecting paint from sun & rain

    As mentioned earlier, applying wax with hydrophobic effects can help repel water, avoiding water spots. A whole car cover is recommended for long-term storage, especially if your car is parked outside, unprotected by roofing and walls.

Seasonal Care

  • Rainy season protection

    It is recommended to dry your car after a rain downpour using a high GSM plush car drying towel to avoid water spots embedding onto the car paint, damaging it in the long run and will worsen if left untreated.

  • Summer heat maintenance

    Wash more frequently in summer to remove dust and pollen buildup, especially if kept outside and without a cover of some sort. Going back to the interior, UV damage can happen. Remember to always apply UV protectant-infused dashboard polish to help the plastics and trims from fading and cracking.

  • Cold weather prep

    Using a snow scraper or snow brush to remove heavy build-up of snow can further quicken the removal of snow on your car. If your car is outside, it is advised to get your car inside before snow comes. Ice or icicles can build up on the overhangs of the car and windows. Make sure to remove these first safely or wait for them to melt before accessing the interior.
